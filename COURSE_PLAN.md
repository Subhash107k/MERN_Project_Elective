# 🗓️ 18-Day / 45-Hour MERN Course Master Schedule

The course is structured for **18 days × 2.5 hours per day = 45 total hours**.
Each 2.5-hour day is divided into:
* **1.0 Hour:** Theory & Core Architectural Concepts
* **1.0 Hour:** Guided Live Coding Demonstration
* **0.5 Hour:** Hands-on Practical Exercises & Homework Debugging
